Our Story

Founded in 2024, Sweet Crust Bakery began as a small family-owned shop with a simple goal: to bring the taste of traditional, slow-fermented bread to our neighborhood.

Our Mission

To consistently provide our customers with high-quality, delicious baked goods while supporting local farmers and creating a warm, welcoming community space.

Our Vision

To be the most beloved local bakery known for our craftsmanship, community involvement, and exceptional customer service.
